Lexicon Curator Engine

Load a lexicon, look up definitions, curate them, and export an immutable, content-addressed JSON-LD graph — all in this browser tab, no server.

Definitions are fetched live from the free dictionaryapi.dev (Wiktionary, CC BY-SA) — the §4.2 adapter. Pick a sense per term to select it, or mark it reject/ambiguous (§3.2), then export. Everything else runs in-tab: normalize (§4.1) · status & phases (§3) · validate incl. credential scan (§7) · content-addressing (§5).
